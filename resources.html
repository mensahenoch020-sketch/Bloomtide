Resources & FAQ

Understand the appraisal process before you begin.

Answers to common questions about appraisal requests, inspections, fees, timelines and how Bloomtide reviews assignments.

Appraisal basics

A real estate appraisal is an opinion of value developed for a specific assignment, intended use and effective date using relevant property and market information.

Value is assignment-specific

The same property may be appraised for different purposes or effective dates. The scope and intended use matter.

Inspection is only one part

When an inspection is part of the assignment, it provides property information. Market research and analysis remain central to the valuation process.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an appraisal cost?

Fees vary by property, location, complexity, intended use and assignment scope. Bloomtide reviews the request before setting the applicable fee.

How long does an appraisal take?

Timing depends on the assignment, inspection scheduling, complexity and information required. Bloomtide confirms expected timing after reviewing the request.

What should I prepare for an inspection?

Provide access to the property and any relevant information requested for the assignment. Specific preparation instructions can be confirmed when scheduling.

Do you accept requests throughout the United States?

Bloomtide accepts requests nationwide. Actual assignment acceptance depends on location, scope, appraiser eligibility and applicable state licensing requirements.

Can I choose my own appraisal fee?

No. Bloomtide determines the fee after reviewing the assignment requirements.

Can an appraisal be done for a past date?

Some assignments may call for a retrospective effective date. Availability depends on the purpose, scope and available information.

Before requesting

Three questions to consider.

What property?

Why is value needed?

The purpose helps determine whether Bloomtide can accept the assignment and what scope may be appropriate.

When is it needed?

Share any deadline and your preferred inspection timing when submitting the request.

Submit the property details and Bloomtide will review the assignment.
