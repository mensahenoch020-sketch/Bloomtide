Meet the founder

Charles Robinson

Charles Robinson founded Bloomtide with a clear purpose: to bring clarity, professionalism and confidence to real estate valuation. His approach centers on careful analysis, strong client service and helping property owners and other stakeholders better understand the factors that influence real property value.

At Bloomtide, Charles believes an appraisal should provide more than a number—it should provide useful insight clients can understand and rely upon when making important real estate decisions.
