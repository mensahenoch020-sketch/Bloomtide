Our services

Valuation services for important property decisions.

Bloomtide supports residential, commercial, land and specialized appraisal needs with careful analysis, clear communication and assignment-specific research.

01

Residential Appraisals

Valuation for single-family homes, condominiums, townhomes and other residential properties for sale, purchase, planning and other permitted purposes.

02

Commercial Appraisals

Analysis for offices, retail, multifamily and other commercial property assignments where objective market-supported value is needed.

03

Land & Vacant Property

Valuation of residential lots, undeveloped land and other land interests with consideration of location, use, access and market activity.

04

Estate & Probate

Appraisal support for estate administration, probate and planning matters where a defensible property value may be required.

05

Divorce & Legal Support

Independent property valuation intended to help clients and their professional advisers understand real estate value in appropriate legal matters.

06

Pre-Listing & Pre-Purchase

Independent value insight for owners and buyers who want a clearer picture before listing, purchasing or negotiating property.

07

Investment Analysis

Property and market analysis for investors evaluating holdings, acquisition opportunities or real estate-related decisions.

08

Retrospective Valuations

Valuation developed as of a prior effective date when assignment conditions and available market evidence make that work appropriate.

09

Assessment Review Support

Independent valuation analysis that can help property owners understand an assessed value and discuss next steps with appropriate professionals.
