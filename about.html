About Bloomtide

Clear valuation. Thoughtful service.

Bloomtide was created to make real estate appraisal feel more understandable, responsive and professional from the first request through the valuation process.

Founder & Owner

Charles Robinson

Charles Robinson founded Bloomtide with a clear purpose: to bring clarity, professionalism and confidence to real estate valuation.

His approach centers on careful analysis, strong client service and helping property owners and other stakeholders better understand the factors that influence real property value.

At Bloomtide, Charles believes an appraisal should provide more than a number. It should provide useful insight clients can understand when making important real estate decisions.

Our approach

Built around four principles.

Clarity

We aim to communicate the appraisal process, assignment requirements and next steps in understandable terms.

Professionalism

Every client interaction should be organized, respectful and focused on the purpose of the assignment.

Careful analysis

Valuation work depends on property-specific information, market evidence and appropriate appraisal methodology.

Client service

From the first request through scheduling and status updates, responsive communication matters.

Why Bloomtide

Property value changes with context.

The Bloomtide name reflects two ideas: bloom as growth and value becoming visible, and tide as the movement of markets over time.

Our role is to help clients understand property value within the context of the assignment, the property and the market.

Service coverage
Bloomtide accepts appraisal requests across the United States. Assignment acceptance depends on property location, scope, appraiser eligibility and applicable state licensing requirements.
